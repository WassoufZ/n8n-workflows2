## What it is (read this first)

My Crystal Code (MCC) sells a **personalized digital reading**, not physical crystals.

- The buyer answers a **12-question questionnaire** (4 sections: Energy & Rhythm, Expression &
  Receiving, Inner Truth & Body, Self-perception) plus birth details.
- An AI pipeline turns the answers into a **private web report delivered by email within minutes**:
  energy story, the seven energy centers (chakras), personal patterns, beliefs, a
  four-crystal protocol, a seven-day reset, and more.
- **Nothing physical ships.** Crystals are "intentional, symbolic companions rather than
  guaranteed solutions."
- Bilingual: **English and Arabic** (`/ar`, RTL, feminine-gender Arabic support). Support is a UAE
  WhatsApp number, so the Gulf/Arabic-speaking market matters as much as the English one.

## Audiences (the site's own four segments)

1. **Pattern explorer** — "You notice the pattern. But what keeps it going?" (overthinking,
   boundaries, people-pleasing).
2. **Energy-curious** — knows the 7 chakras, wants to know *theirs*.
3. **Crystal enthusiast** — "What if choosing a crystal started with understanding yourself?"
4. **Self-discovery seeker** — "Something feels out of sync, but you can't put it into words."

Plus the **money angle** (Full only): "What if your money block isn't where you think it is?"
Loop: *a need → a belief → a feeling → a response → a result.*

## Voice

Warm, intelligent, reflective, a little poetic; invites rather than prescribes. Second person.
Curious questions over claims. Never woo-woo hype, never fear-based. Premium, calm, not loud.

## Hard compliance rules (non-negotiable, from the site's own disclaimers)

- "Reflective guidance, **not medical, psychological, or financial advice**."
- Energy-center interpretations are "**reflective and spiritual, not medical measurements or diagnoses**."
- **Never** claim crystals heal, cure, treat anxiety/depression, attract money, or guarantee outcomes.
- The Abundance Pattern is "**not a prediction of your financial future**."
- No fake testimonials, no invented reader stories presented as real. "Lina" is always labeled
  as an illustrative/fictional sample.
- Written for self-aware **adults**.
